# MekongPulse — feature scan of comparable systems (internal note)

Researched 18 Oct 2026. Every URL below was accessed 2026-10-18. Code paths refer to `projects/` in this repo. `Inference:` marks reasoning, not sourced fact. Scope: only features serving farmers, government officials, local sellers/traders, or the public.

## 1. System inventory

### 1.1 Google Flood Hub
- Audience/channel: public with no signup via Google Search, Maps and Android notifications (Google app + location on; "Crisis Alerts" notification category) (support.google.com/sosalerts); opt-in per-country daily email (support.google.com/flood-hub/answer/16499229); web expert layer for governments/NGOs/researchers (blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/advanced-flood-hub-features-for-aid-organizations-and-governments/).
- Key features: free 7-day riverine forecasts, updated daily, 150+ countries (cities.google/flood-hub); daily email lists significant flood events + high-confidence alerting gauges with severity and past-event links; gauge thresholds are 2-/5-/20-year return levels outside countries where the official agency sets them (answer/16499229); expert mode adds a layers panel, inundation probability, inundation history (limited lat/long), an "additional coverage" toggle for lower-confidence gauges (answer/15637289), a per-gauge basin view "to identify the best forecast location for tracking such as a village, hospital or shelter" (blog.google 2025-02-18), and a side panel with hydrograph, gauge metadata, verified-model flag, inundation-map availability and thresholds (answer/15636998); separate flash-flood model with its own coverage map (answer/16811681); Floods API by waitlist + historical datasets Inundation History 1999–2020 and GRRR 1980–2023 (developers.google.com/flood-forecasting).
- Gaps/borrow: riverine only, no crop or agronomy layer, no two-way contact, public notification needs a smartphone with the Google app; GiveDirectly embedded the API in an anticipatory-action system for village-level alerts and cash transfers (blog.google 2025-02-18). Borrow: country/area daily digest email; confidence labels + coverage toggle; basin view.

### 1.2 MRC portal / One Mekong app / MRC channels
- Audience/channel: agencies first (email + web bulletins to National Mekong Committees, line agencies, CSOs, media), public second (One Mekong app; weekly TV on Facebook/YouTube).
- Key features: RFDMC forecasts 22 mainstream points from 138 stations, daily bulletins June–October, flash-flood guidance twice daily, weekly/monthly drought outlooks; the FFW site updates daily in flood season and Mondays in the dry season (mrcmekong.org/flood-and-drought-forecasting; mrcmekong.org/flood-and-drought; ffw.mrcmekong.org/aboutus.php). The data portal carries an explicit-limitations notice: "The station reading alone cannot tell you whether an area is flooded. Some low-lying areas may flood before our measurements… reach alarm level" and "the accuracy of predictions decreases with each day away from the recorded level" (portal.mrcmekong.org/monitoring/flood-forecasting). One Mekong app: push alerts for flood/drought/weather/disaster, iReport community reports with photos/pin/comments, live CCTV at stations, a ChatGPT-backed chatbot ("Nong Nam"), custom map layers; launched 23 Dec 2024 (mrcmekong.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/MRC-mobile-app-Eng-version.pdf; mrcmekong.org/pr-06012025). Pre-launch coverage described push alerts in four languages and listening to "voice broadcasts" (phnompenhpost.com/national/post-in-depth/mrc-to-release-new-real-time-mekong-monitoring-app/). Weekly Flood & Drought Forecast TV runs on Facebook/YouTube with Khmer/Thai/Vietnamese captions (linkedin.com/posts/mrcsecretariat, 13 Jan 2026). No official MRC Telegram channel found; re-dissemination is via NMCs/media, and the Telegram-group habit belongs to EWS 1294 (research/landscape.md §1.1).
- Borrow: iReport-style two-way reporting; live station/CCTV view; a visible "how to read this / limitations" panel.

### 1.3 Cambodia EWS 1294 (PIN + NCDM)
- Audience/channel: citizens register by dialing 1294 (province/district/commune); delivery by IVR voice call, Telegram groups, ABC 107.5 radio, loudspeakers and SMS; operators are PCDM/NCDM officials (peopleinneed.net/cambodias-early-warning-system-1294-8693gp; peopleinneed.net/from-fear-to-fortitude-ews-1294-empowers-at-risk-communities-in-battambang-13322gp; undrr.org/resource/case-study/mobile-technology-expanding-inclusive-early-warning-communication).
- Key features: operator dashboard where an official picks the target location and sends either a pre-recorded template or their own recorded voice; CAP-conformant since Aug 2020 so one alert feeds several channels; sensors automatically phone assigned focal points as levels near thresholds (resources.peopleinneed.net/documents/1055-1.pdf); loudspeaker dissemination status is visible in real time on the platform (peopleinneed.net/disseminating-early-warning-messages-8460gp); ~190,000 registered users and 41 sensors in Jan 2024 (phnompenhpost.com/national/early-warning-system-readies-citizens-for-natural-disasters/); >2M alerts to 125,000+ subscribers by 2022 (peopleinneed.net/disseminating-early-warning-messages-8460gp); 25,000 registered in Siem Reap with ~80% relaying the warning onward, but post-event reviews found remote-coverage gaps (undrr.org case study).
- Borrow: operator templates + target-area selection with live delivery status; the "who is registered where" coverage metric.

### 1.4 IRRI Rice Crop Manager, AfricaRice RiceAdvice, AKILIMO
- Audience/channel: extension workers, crop advisors, input/service providers interviewing farmers; outputs as one-page printout or SMS, offline-capable.
- RCM inputs: field location, variety, planting date, crop establishment, water source/status, harvest method; output: fertilizer products, amounts and timings for a target yield; next steps named by IRRI are weather-forecast integration and LLM voice in local languages (ricetoday.irri.org/from-science-to-scale-how-rice-crop-manager-is-revolutionizing-farming-for-millions/; irri.org/crop-manager). Stage placement is derived: variety growth duration from a stored database + seedling age sets N application days after transplanting for early vegetative, mid-tillering and panicle initiation (doi.org/10.1016/j.fcr.2019.107578).
- RiceAdvice: 5–10 minute interview covering rice-growing environment, practices, expected sowing date, fertilizer availability/price, budget and target yield; output is a fertilizer plan + crop calendar with days-after-sowing windows + expected income; works offline; >60,000 advisories; printout path for farmers without smartphones (africarice.org/riceadvice; riceadvice.info/en/; africarice.org PDF "RiceAdvice" bundle).
- AKILIMO: Android app plus printable advisory sheets; site-specific fertilizer, intercropping and scheduled-planting guidance from farm characteristics and management choices (portal.akilimo.org/index.php/advisory-tools/).
- Borrow: stage derived from planting date + variety duration rather than asked; printable/offline artifacts; the service-provider (extension) mode.

### 1.5 Conversational and Khmer-language advisory tools
- Audience/channel: farmers and extension agents on mobile chat/apps; Khmer-language examples all run on Telegram.
- FarmerChat: voice, text and photo input; answers as text, audio or video; weather widget (7-day glance, tap for 3-day narrative); image diagnosis; market prices; Telegram/WhatsApp/IVR distribution; 4,500+ extension agents (Jan 2024) (digitalgreen.org/farmerchat; openai.com/index/digital-green; ekam.isdm.org.in/dm/ask-and-they-receive/); an independent 450-farmer study reports NPS 63 and values "accurate weather updates", while criticising generic and overly long answers (60decibels.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/25.11.03_Digital-Green-Farmer.Chat_Report_Final_Updated.pdf).
- Plantix: photo diagnosis, district-level disease alerts, community Q&A, agricultural weather guidance ("best time to weed, spray and harvest"), fertilizer calculator, and a crop calendar built from the user's crop and planting time (plantix.net/en/; play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.peat.GartenBank; plantix.net/en/imprint/terms-and-conditions/).
- Khmer tools: ChivCheat — Khmer Telegram chatbot on Gemini, images/voice/text, cashew focus; 60% of farmers in the Kampong Thom pilot area already used Telegram (cgiar.org/news-events/news/turning-cashew-dreams-into-reality-chivcheats-ai-chatbot-on-its-way-to-transform-cambodian-agriculture). Krova Agri — Khmer-first Telegram assistant (text, voice, photos) in research/pilot (krova.org). Khmer Smart Farming — hourly + 10-day forecasts, crop information, an embedded 1294 EWS voice-alert button, agro-met recommendations, advisory calls/SMS (dvi-ke.fao.org/digital_solutions_details.php?id=300). Sokhapheapdamnam (iDE) — Khmer/English plant-health app, ~6,000 farmers (dvi-ke.fao.org/digital_solutions_details.php?id=471).
- Inference: Khmer Telegram chat is now a crowded lane; MekongPulse's edge remains hazard × stage, not general agronomy.

### 1.6 FAO/MOWRAM PEARL agromet bulletins + Cambodian price bulletins
- Audience/channel: farmers in four northern Tonle Sap provinces via Telegram and Facebook; officials/programmes via the CAS website.
- PEARL (FAO + MAFF/MOWRAM, GCF-funded): monthly agrometeorological bulletin with rainfall, sunlight, heat accumulation and climate projections plus crop-specific advice (cashew, organic rice, mango, leafy vegetables), targeting 450,000 farmers; 8 new and 12 upgraded agro-met stations (cambodianess.com/article/weather-alerts-boost-yields-for-farmers; fao.org/newsroom/story/pearls-of-wisdom-help-to-climate-proof-cambodian-farms/en; fao.org/cambodia/news/detail/new-weather-monitoring-stations-help-cambodian-farmers-adapt-to-climate-change/en; reliefweb.int/report/cambodia/agromet-bulletin-issue-1-october-2024-enkm; cas.appchamka.com).
- Market prices, MAFF AMIS/CAMAgriMarket: wholesale prices three times a week for 256 products, retail weekly from ~30 markets; the app connects farmers with buyers/sellers; monthly price bulletins are published (phnompenhpost.com/business/agricultural-market-information-service-building-inclusive-business-ecosystem/; amis.maff.gov.kh/document/monthly-price-bulletin; amret.com.kh/en/blog/amret-and-maff-use-the-latest-digital-technology-to-empower-farmers). WFP/MAFF's Food Price and Wage Bulletin adds terms-of-trade context (docslib.org/doc/3361223/cambodia-food-price-and-wage-bulletin-...).
- Borrow: monthly cadence for non-urgent content; explicit crop lists per province; harvest-time price context.

### 1.7 Damage reporting, coverage verification, anticipatory-action triggers
- One Mekong iReport lets citizens file flood/drought/pollution reports with photos, pin and comments (MRC app PDF, §1.2). WFP PRISM fuses satellite hazard indicators, crowd-sourced prices and household surveys; district officials were trained to collect loss-and-damage data on an open-source mobile collection app, and NCDM used PRISM for rapid assessments of the Sept–Oct floods; rollout covers all 25 provinces (docs.wfp.org/api/documents/WFP-0000137807/download/; wfpidn.github.io/prism-docs/; wfp.org/publications/leveraging-climate-risk-data-tools-inform-social-protection-case-wfps-platform-real).
- IFRC forecast-based financing: pre-agreed triggers release funds automatically; protocols must include a "stop mechanism" if the forecast improves; the Philippines flood protocol covers four basins with a 72-hour lead time and actions including early harvesting of matured crops and livestock evacuation; the Bangladesh protocol uses a two-stage trigger (GloFAS 10-day, 50% probability, pre-activation; FFWC 5-day deterministic activation at DL+0.85 m for three days) with ODK-based monitoring (adore.ifrc.org/Download.aspx?FileId=288701; ifrc.org/happening-now/emergencies/anticipatory-pillar-dref).
- WFP Cambodia + RIMES: Pursat basin AAP trigger based on the rainfall-to-Bak-Trakoun-level relationship linked to 3/5/10-year return periods; readiness trigger up to 7 days, activation 3 days; targets IDPoor households; 2025 monsoon pilot (reliefweb.int "10 Years of Action — Anticipatory Action — Year in Focus 2024"). FAO reached 1.7M people with 2023–24 El Niño anticipatory action including Cambodia (openknowledge.fao.org, "El Niño 2023–2024: Evidence from FAO's anticipatory action interventions"). Anticipation Hub lists six Cambodian AA frameworks and four activations (anticipation-hub.org/global-overview/countries/cambodia).
- Borrow: name our tiers as readiness/activation with a stop mechanism, and publish a machine-readable trigger feed partners can wire into a protocol.

## 3. Deliberately excluded from the roadmap

- SMS/cell-broadcast or IVR stack: NCDM/PIN/GSMA own it and are mid-migration to broadcast (undrr.org case study). MekongPulse should hand off, not rebuild.
- General agronomy chatbot and photo pest diagnosis: RCM/RiceAdvice/FarmerChat/Plantix/ChivCheat/Krova already cover it; our differentiation is hazard × stage, not crop knowledge breadth.
- Hazard-only dashboard: MRC, Flood Hub and MekongFlow publish the raw layer; our console earns its place only through the stage-specific, official-facing workflow above.
