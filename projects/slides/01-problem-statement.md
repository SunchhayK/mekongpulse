# Problem statement

**Flood forecasts stop at the river. Farmers need the next action.**

- Cambodia's Lower Mekong Basin: 152,386 ha of farmland hit in 2022. 85,482 households. 15 deaths.
- US$405-500/ha of wet-season rice production cost at risk in a lost season.
- About 28% of indebted rural households pledged land titles. Crop loss becomes land loss.

## The gap

- MRC and Google Flood Hub publish river levels in meters. No crop stage. No field instruction.
- EWS 1294 sends generic hazard voice calls. No agronomic guidance.
- Text PDFs and English bulletins exclude low-literacy smallholders.

**MekongPulse turns live telemetry into commune-level, crop-stage-specific Khmer voice instructions.**
